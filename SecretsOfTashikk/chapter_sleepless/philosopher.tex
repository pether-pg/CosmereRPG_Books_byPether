\section{Philosopher}
\label{sec:philosopher}

When the PCs approach the man sitting in the yard of the orphanage, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	When you approach an amphitheater nearby the orphanage, you see a lone man wrapped in shiqua sitting on one of the benches. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"Hello, stranger", he says. "Please, approach. My name is \npcArclo{} and I am a philosopher. Perhaps you can help me? Please, answer me this - what part of a body are you?"
}

The philosopher invites the PCs into a philosophical discussion. You may run this scene as a Conversation.

\npcArclo{} is a \enemySleeplessC{}. \conversationDefence{He}{18}{19}{6}
He enjoys philosophical conversations and will always appreciate a thought-provoking answer.

\conversationEnd{\npcArclo}{1}

\subsection{Aftermath}

If the PCs succeeded to satisfy the philosopher's curiosity, later in the scene they will be invited to take care of a cremling - see \fullref{sec:unexpectedHelp}.

However, before that happens, allow the scene to continue with \fullref{ssec:philosopherAnswers}.

\ptrBox{
	Winning a philosophical debate might be recognized by an inkspren.
}


\subsection{Repay with Answers}
\label{ssec:philosopherAnswers}

Proceed with reading the following:

\quoteBox{
	"You answered my questions, stranger", the philosopher says. "It is the only fair I now answered yours. Tell me, how can I help you?"
}
\newpage

\npcArclo{} offers to answers the questions that the PCs might have. If they wish, they may stay and talk with him.

\scriptedOption{Who are you?}{
	As I told you, I am just a thinker. I like to sit here, observe the world and work on a philosophy.
}

\scriptedOption{Do you know many people in this city?}{
	I spoke with many. I observed many. But I cannot say I know them very well... Not many people wish to stop and talk. There was this girl, but she is gone now. And \npcAxies{}, but he probably lost our bet and is now imprisoned or exiled...
}

\scriptedOption{Can we help your friend?}{
	If you see him, most probably in some prison, tell him I said "I told you so!"
}

\scriptedOption{What did your friend do?}{
	He was sure he can steal \locationYeddaw{} secrets. They are all gathered in \locationIndicium{}, but the people of \locationTashikk{} guard their secrets well.
}

\scriptedOption{Can you help us? We are looking for a Singer.}{
	Many of those were exiled from the city, right before the new storm. I understand the storm made them hostile. They are not welcome back here.
}

\scriptedOption{What happened in that orphanage?}{
	\textit{If the PCs succeeded in the Conversation against \npcArclo{}:}
	A woman was running it. Her truthfulness attracted an ancient power. She now left this place to serve the emperor and the world.
}

\reasonBox{
	During this conversation, make sure \npcArclo{} mentions those two clues: 1) \npcAxies{} being imprisoned for attempted stealing of secrets and 2) \locationYeddaw{} secrets being guarded in \locationIndicium{}. The PCs may need those two leads to proceed with the story.
}
