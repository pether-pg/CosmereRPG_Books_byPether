\section{Blind Cultist}
\label{sec:2ndMeeting}

The party meets \npcBlindBeggar{} for the 2nd time right after \fullref{ssec:stormshelterTradeSecrets}. When they do, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	As you step away from the stormshelter, you see a figure standing in the nearby shadow. Thick cords are sewing his eyelids shut, and his teeth shine bare in a mischievous smile. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"So, you are looking for a Parshman", \npcBlindBeggar{} says. "That's intriguing... You know all of them were exiled from the city just before that red storm, right? It would be illegal for one to stay behind!\vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"And that secret you want to give him? What does it say, hm...?"
}

\npcBlindBeggar{}, the \enemyCorruptedLightweaverC{}, is now aware that the PCs are looking for a Singer. That intrigues him and makes him want to learn more about the PCs and their motivation. He starts talking with the party with a plan to use his abilities and steal their secrets.

Depending on the PCs' previous interaction with him, he will approach this conversation in a different way. If the PCs shared a secret with him during their previous meeting, he will try recruiting them to his cult - proceed to \fullref{ssec:2ndMeetingRecruit}. However, if the PCs showed hostility, he will respond with the same attitude - proceed to \fullref{ssec:2ndMeetingThreaten}.

\reasonBox{
	If \npcBlindBeggar{} manages to steal a secret from a PC using his abilities, make a note of that. He may use them against that PC to make them lose Focus during a Conversation, but he might hold on to some of them for a later use. During \fullref{sec:3rdMeeting} you will need to know how many secrets does he start the scene with.
}


\subsection{Cult Recruitment}
\label{ssec:2ndMeetingRecruit}

During this scene, \npcBlindBeggar{} will attempt to tempt the party, promising them he will help them to join the Cult of Secrets and develop new magical powers, allowing the PCs to easily learn the secrets held by the informant.

He also says that his friends are spread across the city, and will have no difficulty finding anyone, making the search for \npcInformant{} a much easier task.

The price to join the cult, however, is to give up \lootSealedMessage{} to \npcBlindBeggar{}. If the party accepts this deal, proceed to \fullref{sec:cultHideout}.


\subsection{Threaten Secrets}
\label{ssec:2ndMeetingThreaten}

During this scene, \npcBlindBeggar{} will attempt to blackmail the PCs using their secret against them. His goal is to obtain \lootSealedMessage{} the PCs are protecting.

You may run this scene as a Conversation. However, if the PCs don't try to persuade him, there is no need to invoke Conversation rules.

\npcBlindBeggar{} uses the \enemyCorruptedLightweaverC{} stat block. \conversationDefence{He}{16}{20}{7}
Hungry for secrets, he will need to spend an additional Focus to resist influence attempts revealing one of them.

During the Conversation, \npcBlindBeggar{} will use his abilities to steal 1 secret from each of the PCs. After that, he will threaten to expose them, selling the information to the authorities or interested parties.

\conversationEnd{\npcBlindBeggar{}}{1}
The Conversation also ends when the PCs give him \lootSealedMessage{}. He will see through any attempt of deception and will accept only the real one.

If instead of the Conversation the PCs initiate Combat, proceed to \fullref{ssec:2ndMeetingCombat}.

\reasonBox{
	During this scene, make sure to show that \npcBlindBeggar{} can be dangerous to the PCs without ever drawing a weapon. However, do not allow him to steal all the secrets, leave something for \fullref{sec:3rdMeeting}.
}




\subsection{Armed Combat}
\label{ssec:2ndMeetingCombat}

If the PCs attack \npcBlindBeggar{}, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	"Drawing weapons against a poor beggar...? Manslaughter is illegal, you must know that, right?" \npcBlindBeggar{} asks. "Well, I'm sorry to disappoint you, but I cannot satisfy you in this very moment... I have a Parshman to find. Another day, perhaps...?"
}

\npcBlindBeggar{} will not attempt to fight the party, yet. He will Disengage and Move away from the PCs, using Regenerate to heal any damage. If possible, he will attempt to steal some of the party's secrets, but he will not risk his life for that.

Once \npcBlindBeggar{} reaches a nearby street, he will use Illumination to blend into the crowd, disappearing from sight.

\reasonBox{
	Make sure \npcBlindBeggar{} survives this encounter, he will be needed for the final boss fight in \fullref{sec:3rdMeeting}.
}
