\section{Skybreaker Constables}
\label{enemyFractionSkybreakers}

Following the teachings of Herald Nale, the Skybreakers serve in Azish princedoms as protectors of the law and justice. This includes ensuring the recent prohibition of using supernatural powers enacted by the Prince of \locationTashikk{} is properly respected. Due to the Herald’s influence, breaking that law is punished by death and non-Radiant law enforcement officers may be overwhelmed by the Invested perpetrator, hence the necessity to use Skybreakers’ support.

\locationYeddaw{} welcomes their help with gratitude, but their services are not requested against any petty crime happening within city borders - those are handled by City Guards, who call for Skybreaker Constables’ support only in rare cases including exceptionally dangerous criminals or surgebinders.\\


\tacticsBox{
	During Combat, Skybreakers fight to kill. Their appearance means that the City Guard abandoned any hope of non-lethal resolution of the conflict and the only way to ensure the safety for \locationYeddaw{} citizens is to deal with the threat quickly. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	Skybreaker Constables are granted leave to use the surge of Gravitation when facing against dangerous foes, but only a few of them are allowed to use Division.
	}
