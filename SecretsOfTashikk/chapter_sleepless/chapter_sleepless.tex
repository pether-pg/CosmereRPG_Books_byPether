\setcounter{section}{-1}
\section{Running This Chapter}

Sitting nearby the orphanage, \npcArclo{} the philosopher might be helpful for the PCs. This chapter describes how to run scenes with him.

\subsection{Character Advancement}

The characters do not advance to the higher levels during this chapter.

The following events may affect their path to radiance:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Impressing the philosopher in a debate.
	\item Protecting helpless cremling.
\end{itemize}


\subsection{Notable NPCs}

\npcArclo{} is an ancient sleepless, with his horde mainly staying in \locationYeddaw{}. He is a friend of Radiants and he might provide the party with useful information.

\roleplayBox{\npcArclo{}}{
	\roleplayTips
		{Philosophical, secretive}
		{Trade some information for a stimulating philosophical debate.}
		{\npcArclo{} wears a shiqua, a long sheet wrapped around the body, quite popular in \locationTashikk{}. Hidden in its wraps, it's hard to see his face.}
		{\npcArclo{} is a sleepless - an ancient creature composed of small, cremling-like hordelings. Using them, he can observe many things at once. Remembering the previous Desolations, \npcArclo{} wishes to help Radiants in their war against \npcOdium{}.}
}
