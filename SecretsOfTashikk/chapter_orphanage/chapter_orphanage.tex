\section{Running This Chapter}

While \npcArshqqam{}, also known as the Stump, is away, some Surgeons from \locationKharbranth{} took over the place. Use this chapter when the PCs decide to investigate that.

\spoilerBox{
	This chapter reveals the story of one of the characters from \bookED{}, alongside with the plot twist and follow-up events.
}

\subsection{Character Advancement}

The characters do not advance to the higher levels during this chapter.

The following events may affect their path to radiance:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Compassion towards the orphans.
	\item Learning truth from the kids.
	\item Volunteering to run the orphanage.
\end{itemize}


\subsection{Notable NPCs}

\npcDoctor[s]{} is a \enemyDiagramSurgeonB{}. He and a few other surgeons run the orphanage now, slowly turning it into a hospital to help all people in need, not only the children.

\roleplayBox{\npcDoctor}{
	\roleplayTips
