\section{\lootSpanreedStormwarden{}}

\textbf{Type:} Fabrial (Spanreed) \\
\textbf{Charges:} 3

This spanreed is paired with another one owned by \npcStormwarden{} the stormwarden. He will send regular updates about upcoming storms. The update format can be customized by the owner to suit their needs best.

A pair of spanreeds expend 1 charge for every 5 days they are active. Due to the fact the communication will always arrive before the storm, you can assume the spanreed is recharged after use and is always full of charges.



\section{\lootSpanreedTashikk{}}

\textbf{Type:} Fabrial (Spanreed) \\
\textbf{Charges:} 3

This spanreed is connected to the spanreed hub in \locationTashikk{}. Using it, the owner can contact the hub and ask the operator there to connect with any other owner having a similar fabrial, allowing for a communication with multiple people using just one spanreed.

A pair of spanreeds expend 1 charge for every 5 days they are active.
