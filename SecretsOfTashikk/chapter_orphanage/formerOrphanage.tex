\section{Investigating the Orphanage}
\label{sec:investigateOrphanage}

When the PCs reach the orphanage, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	Following the directions, you approach a building carved into a rocky wall. The writing above the entrance says "Tashi's Light Orphanage", but you see orange flags hanging from the windows - surgeons from \locationKharbranth{} are here, offering their help for the wounded.
}

It seems the orphanage is now in the hands of \locationKharbranth{} doctors. When one of them notices the PCs, he will invite them in. Proceed to \fullref{ssec:investigateOrphanageHeal}.


\subsection{Medical Help}
\label{ssec:investigateOrphanageHeal}

If the PCs approach doctors, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	As you enter, you see rows of beds in every room, surgeons in orange robes and white aprons tending their patients. A scribe wearing orange dress approaches you. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"Hello, welcome to our medical facility", she says with a lovely voice. "How can we help you? Are you suffering from wounds? Is your case critical? Doctor \npcDoctor{} will be able to attend to you in just a moment."
}

The PCs may use the service provided by the surgeons: recover Health and heal injuries. The service is free for anyone who needs it, as surgeons form \locationKharbranth{} never charge for the help they provide.

Once his job is completed and all PCs are healed, \npcDoctor{} is ready to proceed to \fullref{ssec:investigateOrphanageSecrets}.

\reasonBox{
	Make sure to run this scene before entering the Conversation with \npcDoctor{}, as some of his skills depend on previous healing provided to the PCs.
}

\subsection{Hospital's Secrets}
\label{ssec:investigateOrphanageSecrets}

When the party inquires about the events leading to \locationKharbranth{} taking over the orphanage, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	"I'm sorry", \npcDoctor{} says. "We are here to provide medical help, but I probably shouldn't disclose the information you are asking for... It's all rumors, you see..."
}

The situation of the orphanage seems to be a secret \npcDoctor{} does not wish to disclose. However, the PC may try to convince him to share it. You can run this scene as a Conversation.

\npcDoctor{} is a \enemyDiagramSurgeonB{}. \conversationDefence{He}{18}{15}{5} He is assisted by a \enemyDiagramScribe{} - she will not participate in the Conversation, but she will provide additional charges of \abilityFutureKnowledgeName[4].

\locationKharbranth{} has sent \npcDoctor{} here to heal as many people as he can and note whatever he learns from his patients. The building of orphanage is a good place for this service and he intends to fully transform it into a hospital, leaving only sick kids in here.

\conversationEnd{\npcDoctor}{1}

\subsubsection{Resolving the Conversation}

If the PCs succeed in convincing the surgeon, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	"OK, perhaps you are right, I won't hurt anyone by telling this", \npcDoctor{} says. "The woman who was working here previously, \npcArshqqam{}, she traveled away. Nobody knows where, but I saw her messages. A Veristitalian from Azimir invited her. Why? The kids say about strange things happening around there, but this cannot be true, can it?\vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"Well, it's good for us, I guess. And for the city. With her gone, we can finally open a proper medical facility. We always struggled to find a proper place, but this city is in desperate need for some surgeons from \locationKharbranth{}. Especially after the recent events... We have so much work in here..."
}

The PCs learn that the woman who formerly ran this place, \npcArshqqam{}, decided to move to Azimir, where she was invited by Veristitalians. It is also possible she was one of the people with strange powers and did not want to break the local law by using them to heal the kids.

With \npcArshqqam{} gone, \npcDoctor{} applied for a permit to use the space, which he hopes to fully transform into a hospital.

The PCs can give use this secrets once they proceed to \fullref{ssec:stormshelterTradeSecrets}.

\subsubsection{Aftermath}

If the PCs failed the Conversation, they might consider interviewing the children to see if they would be willing to share something. If they do, proceed to \fullref{sec:learnFromKids}.

Once the conversation is over, regardless of the result, proceed to \fullref{ssec:investigateOrphanageKid}.


\subsection{Curious Orphan}
\label{ssec:investigateOrphanageKid}

Before the PCs leave the orphanage, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	As you are about to leave, you see a small girl approaching you. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"Hello, big lowd!", she says to you. "Awe you hewe to talk with the smawt man? He is vewy smawt, you know? He knows so much stuff! But he is not hewe, he always sits ovew thewe!"
}

\reasonBox{
	The girl has some spelling problems, pronouncing "R" as "W". If you have trouble reading her line with all those typos, here is the corrected version: "Hello, big lord! Are you here to talk with the smart man? He is very smart, you know? He knows so much stuff! But he is not here, he always sits over there!".
}

According to the orphan, there is a very wise man spending time in the yard. Or at least - very wise by the kid's standards. Perhaps he knows some secrets he will be willing to share?

The party can now proceed to "\nameref{chap:chapterSleepless}".
