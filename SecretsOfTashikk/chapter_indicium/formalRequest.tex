\section{Formal Request}
\label{sec:formalRequest}

The PCs may attempt to make a formal request to access sensitive information about the whereabouts of the singer informant. To do this, they will need to enter the Conversation with \npcGhenna{} and her assistant.

\npcGhenna[s] is the \enemyScribeC{} of Information, while her assistant is a \enemyScribeB{} of Information. Both of them will contribute to the Conversation, but only \npcGhenna{} must be persuaded.

\conversationDefence{\npcGhenna{}}{18}{17}{6}
She believes in the authority of the law and legal procedures and she will need to spend 2 Focus to resist an influence of a person having an authority she must respect.

All tests made by the PCs gain disadvantage if they cannot present proper immigration documents they could obtain during \fullref{sec:immigrationForm}. 

If \fullref{sec:wanted} is active, raise stakes with every roll the PCs are making. You may spend multiple \iconComp{} during the Conversation to progress the event.

\conversationEnd{\npcGhenna{}}{1}

\reasonBox{
	During the Conversation, both \npcGhenna{} and her assistant can use Form Ash-38 action. For a better flow of the scene, do not allow both of them to be active at the same time and target another character with the 2nd use of the ability.
}

\subsubsection{Aftermath}

If the PCs succeed in the Conversation, they will learn about Singer sightings in the eastern parts of the Immigrant Quarter. With this information they may start searching for him - see \fullref{sec:searchForSinger}.

If the PCs fail, they will need to find another way to get the information.

\ptrBox{
	Following legal procedures to obtain the information may be recognized by a highspren.
}
