\npcBlindBeggar[s] is a \enemyCorruptedLightweaverC{}. He starts this scene with 0 Investiture and his goal is to get as much PCs' secrets as he can.


\subsection{Giving Him Spheres}

If the PCs give \npcBlindBeggar{} some spheres, read the following:

	TODO
}

The beggar will inform the party that in \locationYeddaw{}, the real currency are information and secrets. He begs for both. However, he will keep the sphere and use its light to use his Lure with Secrets talent, taunting a PC with his findings.


\subsection{Giving Him Secrets}

If the party shares some secrets with him, he will greedily listen to it. If the secret was big enough, it may change his attitude towards the party in the later stages of the story. 

However, the secret must be true. \npcBlindBeggar{} will easily see through any lies. When this happens, he will despise the party, growing more hostile in the later parts of the adventure.
