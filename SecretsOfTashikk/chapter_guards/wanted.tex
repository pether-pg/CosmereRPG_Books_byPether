\section{Wanted!}
\label{sec:wanted}

The people of \locationYeddaw{} treat the law seriously and will not allow any crime of the PCs to go unanswered. If at any point the party commits a crime, announce the start of the following event: \\

\eventComplication{Wanted!}{
	\iconCompEvent
	\iconCompEvent
	\iconCompEvent
}

\subsection{The Story Behind}

In \locationYeddaw{}, information is a valuable coin. People trade with it for money, favors, or other information. The Law Enforcement is no different.

Each time a \iconComp{} is spent for the event, some NPC recognizes the party and sells the information to the city officials. Each piece of information brings them one step closer to catching the PCs. Each time a crime is committed, someone will also tip the authorities.

Once the final \iconComp{} is spent, they track them down and join the scene, ready to detain them.



\subsection{Progressing the Event}

Once per scene, the GM may spend a \iconComp{} from a PC's roll to advance the event's progress. 

On top of that, if the party commits a new crime while the event from the previous one is still in progress, advance the previous one just as if  \iconComp{} was spent.




\subsection{Triggering the Event}

Once the final \iconComp{} is placed, the city guards finally found the criminals they were tracking. For each PC in the party, a \enemyGuardB{} joins the scene. They are lead by \enemyGuardC{}. If the party demonstrated a use of supernatural powers, additional \enemySkybreakerC{} will also be there.

The guards aim to arrest the party. If they do, proceed to \fullref{sec:prison}.

However, if the party previously ran away from the prison, or if they are hostile towards the law enforcement officers, they will determine the prison is no longer a valid option and will attempt to neutralize the criminals.
