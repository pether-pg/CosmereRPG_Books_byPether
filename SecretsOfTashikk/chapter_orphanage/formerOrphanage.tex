It seems the orphanage is now in the hands of \locationKharbranth{} doctors. When one of them notices the PCs, he will invite them in. Proceed to \fullref{ssec:investigateOrphanageHeal}.


\subsection{Medical Help}
\label{ssec:investigateOrphanageHeal}

If the PCs approach doctors, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	TODO
}

The PCs may use the service provided by the surgeons: recover Health and heal injuries. The service is free for anyone who needs it, as surgeons form \locationKharbranth{} never charge for the help they provide.

Once his job is completed and all PCs are healed, \npcDoctor{} is ready to proceed to \fullref{ssec:investigateOrphanageSecrets}.

\reasonBox{
	Make sure to run this scene before entering the Conversation with \npcDoctor{}, as some of his skills depend on previous healing provided to the PCs.
}



\subsection{Hospital's Secrets}
\label{ssec:investigateOrphanageSecrets}

When the party inquires about the events leading to \locationKharbranth{} taking over the orphanage, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	TODO
}

The situation of the orphanage seems to be a secret \npcDoctor{} does not wish to share. However, the PC may try to convince him to share it. You can run this scene as a Conversation.

\npcDoctor{} is \enemyDiagramSurgeonB{}. \conversationDefence{He}{18}{15}{5} He is assisted by a \enemyDiagramScribe{} - she will not participate in the Conversation, but she will provide additional charges of \abilityFutureKnowledgeName[4].

\locationKharbranth{} has sent \npcDoctor{} here to heal as many people as he can and note whatever he learns from his patients. The building of orphanage is a good place for this service and he intends to fully transform it into a hospital, leaving only sick kids in here.

\conversationEnd{\npcDoctor}{1}

\subsubsection{Resolving the Conversation}

If the PCs succeed in convincing the surgeon, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	TODO
}

The PCs learn that the woman who formerly ran this place, \npcArshqqam{}, decided to move to Azimir, where she was invited by Veristitalians. It is also possible she was one of the people with strange powers and did not want to break the local law by using them to heal the kids.

With \npcArshqqam{} gone, \npcDoctor{} applied for a permit to use the space, which he hopes to fully transform into a hospital.

The PCs can give use this secrets once they proceed to \fullref{ssec:stormshelterTradeSecrets}.

\subsubsection{Aftermath}

Once the conversation is over, regardless of the result, proceed to \fullref{ssec:investigateOrphanageKid}.
