\section{Adventure Summary}

Here is a brief summary of the plot of the story. You can use it to quickly familiarize yourself with the adventure events.

Please note, the chapters do not represent the chronological order of the scenes, as the players have freedom to pursue their goals in any order. Instead, they are grouping scenes with similar context together.

\subsection{\nameref{chap:chapter1} - Summary}

The PCs approach \locationYeddaw{} and must complete immigration procedures to enter the city.

\subsection{\nameref{chap:chapterGuards} - Summary}

Those scenes are to be played when the party performs an illegal act and must be held responsible by the local authorities and law enforcement.

\subsection{\nameref{chap:chapterCult} - Summary}

Scenes featuring blind beggar, the \enemyCorruptedLightweaverNameC{} from the local Cult of Secrets worshiping Sja-anat.

\subsection{\nameref{chap:chapterOrphanage} - Summary}

During those scenes, the PCs will attempt to learn the secrets of the local orphanage to trade for other crucial information they need.

\subsection{\nameref{chap:chapterSleepless} - Summary}

Includes scenes for a philosophical debate with a Sleepless and his subsequent offer for the party.

\subsection{\nameref{chap:chapterIndicium} - Summary}

Being a storehouse for the city information, \locationIndicium{} is a great place for the PCs to find some answers and possibly secrets.

\subsection{\nameref{chap:chapterInformant} - Summary}

The PCs' goal for the adventure is to find the informant and trade information with them. Those scenes are used when the PCs are searching for their objective.
