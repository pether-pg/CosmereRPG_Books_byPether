\section{Secret Secured}
\label{sec:missionComplete}

When the PCs learn the secret found by \npcInformant{}, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	The ones who assassinated your niece are called "Ghostbloods". It is a large organization, recognizing their members by a tattoo on their arms: like a triangle made of three diamonds. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	Beware of them, \npcDalinar{}! Beware of anyone dealing with them! They are ruthless invaders from another world. They know of powers we have never heard of, and they will stop at nothing to achieve their goals. And their goals don't care about our world.
}

The party completed their objective! The information is secured, their mission is ended. Victory!

\ptrBox{
	Gathering the information needed by the party's patron may be recognized by a peakspren.
}

\reasonBox{
	The exact content of the obtained information is irrelevant to the story and you can easily replace it with anything else to personalize your adventure.
}
