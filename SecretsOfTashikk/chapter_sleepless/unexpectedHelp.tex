\section{Unexpected Help}
\label{sec:unexpectedHelp}

If the party succeeded on the Conversation in \fullref{sec:philosopher}, read the following before they leave \npcArclo{}:

\quoteBox{
	"I must say, it has been long since I could indulge myself with such stimulating conversation", \npcArclo{} says. "But I have one more request, if you will hear me..." \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	The philosopher reaches to you, and you see a small, purple cremling running on his arm toward you. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"This one yearns to see the world", \npcArclo{} continues. "Please, take care of him, and I will be in your debt."
}

The PCs may accept a cremling as a token of friendship with \npcArclo{}. If they do, he will be willing to come to their help if they would need it - see \fullref{ssec:unexpectedHelpevent}.

\ptrBox{
	Protecting small cremling might be recognized, albeit with a dose of confusion, by an honorspren.}

\reasonBox{The cremling is one of the sleepless' hordeling. Through it, he can see, hear and locate the PCs. However, do not reveal the fact to the players, as most probably their characters would not be aware of that.}

\subsection{Help from the Sleepless}
\label{ssec:unexpectedHelpevent}

If the party accepted the cremling from \npcArclo{}, they can ask him for help later. When they do, start the following event: \\

\event{Unexpected Help}{\iconOppEvent{}\iconOppEvent{}\iconOppEvent{}}{blueRadiance}

\subsubsection{Progressing the Event}

Once per scene, a PC may spend \iconOpp{} to progress the event. That indicates \npcArclo{}'s hordelings finding  new bits of information that may be relevant to the PCs' needs.

\subsubsection{Abandoning the Event}

If at any moment the PCs decide to abandon the cremling or to take hostile actions against it, stop the event and do not allow to restart it later. \npcArclo{} no longer considers the PCs his friends.

\subsubsection{Triggering the Event}

Once the final \iconOpp{} is spent, \npcArclo{} can find the party and tell him the information they need.

%}
