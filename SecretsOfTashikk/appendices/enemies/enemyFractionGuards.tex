\section{Empire Guards}
\label{enemyFractionGuards}

As all princedoms in the Azish Empire, \locationTashikk{} prides themselves on their orderly approach to paperwork, law and bureaucracy. However, they must recognize that even the most perfect laws cannot be enforced without a strong force of guards, maintaining the order.

\locationYeddaw{} does not maintain a big guard garrison. Because of that, their ranks must be augmented with troops from Azir, sent to \locationTashikk{} by the Prime Aqasix to support the local force. After the Everstorm, the demand for the support only increased, as there are many more immigrants converging to the city. \\

\tacticsBox{
	Following the protocol, Azish guards do not seek to exterminate their foes. Instead, they will use non-lethal force in combat, attempting to overwhelm the enemy and detain them in the arrest. However, all armed forces have a leave to use lethal force against any enemy who demonstrated their intentions to use it as well.\vspace{0.15cm}
	
	\hspace*{0.3cm} According to law, all guarding forces are obliged to attempt preventing unauthorized entry before drawing weapons. The guards will inform people what behavior would be considered illegal if they see a risk those people might not be aware. In a Conversation, the guard will use \skillIntimiation{} to influence their adversary, supported by Baleful feature.
	}
