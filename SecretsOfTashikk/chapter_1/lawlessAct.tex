\section{Lawless Entrance}
\label{sec:lawlessAct}

If the party decides to ignore the instructions of the guard and to illegally enter the city, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	As you start running, you hear the guard shouting after you: \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"Hey! Stop! You still didn't present your documentation! Your actions will be deemed illegal!"
}

The party runs past the guards into the city, \npcHauka{} chasing after them. You can run it as a modified Pursuit Endeavor.



\subsection{Pursuit Rules}

Between the guard post and the streets of \locationYeddaw{} there is a Distance of 6 (or 1.5 times the number of PCs). The party starts at Distance 1. Their goal is to get to the city before they are detained by the guards.

\enemyGuardC{} starts at the Distance of 0. In her first turn, \npcHauka{} will call for support, allowing a few more \enemyGuardB{}s to join the scene (enough to match the size of the party).

Starting with the PCs, one character of each team makes a test against a respective Defense of someone in the other party. The PCs always roll against \enemyGuardC{}, the guards can pick their targets. On a success, increase the Distance of the team by 1.
 




\subsection{Resolving the Pursuit}

If the party reached the city first, they can disappear in the crowd, making it impossible for \npcHauka{} to catch them. Proceed to \fullref{sec:enteredCity}.

If \npcHauka{} and the guards managed to catch the PCs, they will be arrested for attempted breaking and a failure to present proper immigration documents. Proceed to \fullref{sec:prison}.

If the party resists the arrest attempt, they will be attacked by \enemyGuardC{} and three \enemyGuardB{}s (or 1 fewer than the number of PCs). If the guards win the fight, proceed to \fullref{sec:prison}. If they are defeated, the PCs can proceed to \fullref{sec:enteredCity}.

\ptrBox{
	Rebelling against any form of containment and subjugation, even if only by law and not a physical bars, may be recognized by a lightspren.
}
