\section{The Story So Far}
\label{sec:theStoryBefore}

Before starting the adventure, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	\npcDalinar{} needs your help! You were sent to \locationTashikk{} on his behalf to meet with a mysterious informant, who is in possession of information your patron needs. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	However, in this kingdom, secrets have their price and the information can be used as a currency. Your patron gave you a sealed letter for the informant. You must protect its contents and deliver it for trade.
}

\textbf{Important!} For this story, make sure you worked with the players to introduce secrets for their PCs as described in \fullref{ssec:beforeStartingSecrets}. This is especially important if you play with preexisting party and did not create new PCs for the adventure, possibly skipping this step.

\reasonBox{
	It is expected to use this adventure as a side quest of a bigger story. Feel free to adjust some details of the plot hook and the party's motivation. Make sure the goal remains the same - find informant and deliver sealed secret to them.
}

\subsection{The Informant}

Before the events of this adventure, \npcDalinar{} received the following message:

\quoteBox{
	To \npcDalinar{} Kholin, leader of alethi forces! \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	What happened to your niece at the sea was not an accident. It was a scheme to assassinate the member of a royal family. I know who is behind it and how to recognize them. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	I can trade this information. However, spanreeds are not safe for this. Meet me in \locationYeddaw{}, in the kingdom of \locationTashikk{}. Ask for \npcInformant{} in the southern stormshelter. Bring a secret for trade, or I will not share what I know. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	Hurry, \npcDalinar{}! Lives of your entire family are at lethal risk!
}

At the time, there were more pressing matters and he could not spare the resources to pursue this thread. Now, however, he decided to sent the PCs with secrets to trade.

The PCs must find the informant in \locationYeddaw{}, trade \lootSealedMessage{} for information he gathered and return with it to \locationUrithiru{}.

The PCs are carrying \lootSealedMessage{} - a secret information to be traded for informant's secrets. 

The PCs are not supposed to open it, and you should be able to run the entire adventure without the need to know its contents, but if ever the PCs decide to see what happens if they break the seal, it contains the following text:

However, opened \lootSealedMessage{} is worthless to the informant. The PCs will need to find another way to get what they need.

\reasonBox{
	The contents of the \lootSealedMessage{} are not important for the plot. Feel free to adjust the text to your needs.
