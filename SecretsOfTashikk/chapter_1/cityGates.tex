\section{City Gates}
\label{sec:cityGates}

When the characters approach the city of \locationYeddaw{}, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	Finally, you arrived in \locationYeddaw{}. In the distance, you see a city carved into the rocky ground, looking like a smaller brother of the Shattered Plains. The main road leading to it is crowded with people - a seemingly endless stream, full of immigrants waiting to enter the city.
}

The waiting line is quite long. Give the PCs some time to interact with each other and surrounding NPCs, before you proceed to \fullref{ssec:cityGatesImmigrant}.



\subsection{Immigrant in Need}
\label{ssec:cityGatesImmigrant}

Start this scene by reading the following:

\quoteBox{
	You see a person approaching you. Just looking at him you see he didn't sleep well recently - eyes tired, small sticks entangled in his hair. Perhaps he has been waiting in this line for several days now... \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"Please, you have to help me!", he begs you. "They say my immigration form has expired, and my entry is denied... I have family in this city, my daughters need me!" 
}

\npcImmigrant{} approaches the party and asks for their help in entering the city. If they agree, the legal process at the gate will be more difficult, but perhaps \npcImmigrant{} will be able to thank the PCs later...?

After you complete this scene, proceed to \fullref{ssec:cityGatesGuards}.

\ptrBox{
	Helping an immigrant in need may be recognized by a cultivationspren.
}


\subsection{Gate Guards}
\label{ssec:cityGatesGuards}

When the party finally approaches the gate, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	Finally, you approach the guard post. There is a scribe sitting on the nearby bench, while the guard captain in a shining breastplate addresses you:  \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"Welcome to \locationYeddaw{}", she says. "If it is your first time visiting the city, you must provide Immigration Form TI-84 before entering the city. You may acquire an empty form from the \enemyScribeB{} of Immigration on your left. Thank you for your cooperation."
}

\npcHauka[s]{}, a \enemyGuardC{} watches the gate. \npcNissiqqan[s]{}, a \enemyScribeB{} of Immigration, sits nearby, ready to assist, should \npcHauka{} need his help.

The party will quickly learn that entering the city requires filling out the proper paperwork (see \fullref{sec:immigrationForm}). The PCs might also try to talk their way in (see \fullref{sec:scribePersuasion}), or just break inside (see \fullref{sec:lawlessAct}).

\spoilerBox{
	\npcHauka{} and \npcNissiqqan{} at \locationYeddaw{} gates appear in \bookED{}, Chapter 3.
}


\subsubsection{(Optional) Identification Form}

If you start this adventure without a party from the previous games, you might consider roleplay through the process of character creation, turning session zero into a more interactive experience.

Before proceeding to the next scene, allow \npcHauka{} to ask each character some questions about details she needs to fill in her paperwork. Let the players define the following at this point:

\begin{itemize}
	\item Name
	\item Appearance
	\item Occupation
	\item Spoken Languages
	\item Carried Weapons
	\item Special Talents
	\item Available Marks
\end{itemize}

After those are answered, you can proceed with filling the details, like exact attribute scores or total skill modifiers.

\reasonBox{
	Please note, this is not a recommended way to play this scenario as you should probably try to weave it with another module. However, I'm putting the idea here - in case you needed it and found it interesting.
}
