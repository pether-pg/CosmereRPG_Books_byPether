\section{Indicium Services}
\label{sec:indiciumServices}

As the PCs enter \locationIndicium{}, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	As you enter the building, you see a long desk on the other side of the buildings. Behind it, scribes and clerks are sitting, talking with patrons much like yourselves. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	One of them is waving to you. "Welcome to \locationIndicium{}", she says. "How can we be of service?"
}

\locationIndicium{} is the pride of \locationYeddaw{} people, open for everyone to access information they need. On top of that, it offers the following services:

\subsection{Tashikk Spanreed}

Anyone can buy a \lootSpanreedTashikk{} connected to a spanreed hub for 300 mk. Using it, the owner can communicate with everyone having similar fabrial - the operator working with the owner's spanreed will copy the message and send it to the recipient. 

This way, only one fabrial is required to speak with almost anyone in the world, but one must remember the messages sent this way are being made known to third parties, making it unsuitable for critical secrets.

\subsection{Sending a Message}

For a price of 10 mk, anyone can sent a message to a person owning a \lootSpanreedTashikk{}. After their response, each subsequent message sent to them is priced at 5 mk. Each new conversation with another person costs 10 mk.

While long-term benefits of owning \lootSpanreedTashikk{} are much more versatile, this is a cheap option for someone who needs to send a quick message.

\subsection{Basic Research}

For a price of 100 mk, the customer is promised to be delivered extensive information about any documented topic. The customer is not required to know which library stores those information - this is handled by the operators. 

After the service is completed, the customer is given the knowledge notes sent back by the libraries, granting them an Expertise on that topic, as long as they have the notes and are able to read them.

Obscure topics and sensitive information are not covered by the service.
