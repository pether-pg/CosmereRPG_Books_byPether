To enter the city, the party must present proper paperwork. This is a standard procedure across Azish Empire and the end of the world is not an excuse to abandon the law. You can run this scene as an Endeavor.

\pursuitReqPerPC{2}{1} If the party agreed to help the \npcImmigrant{} from \fullref{ssec:cityGatesImmigrant}, they need to earn 2 more successes.



\subsection{Form Language}

Despite the guards speaking multiple languages, the Immigration Form is written in azish. The scribes assume that anyone who applies for immigrant status is required to understand the local language. 

All tests contributing to the Endeavor gain disadvantage if the party does not have a character with Azish Culture Expertise.

On the other hand, if someone in the party has some form of Legal Expertise, they will be able to make sense of the Form, granting advantage to all the tests.

\subsection{Endeavor Contribution}

The DC of the Endeavor tests should fall in the range of 13-16 (12 + 1d4) on tier 2, or (8 + 1d4 + 2 $\times$ tier) on other tiers.

In order to contribute to the Endeavor, the PCs might try one of the suggestions below or propose their own ideas:

\scriptedOption{Law Knowledge.}{
	A character may attempt \skillCheck{14}{\skillLore} to determine if they know enough law relating to the Form. Due to the legal nature of the Endeavor, a successful tests scores two successes towards the Endeavor completion.
}

\scriptedOption{Spy on Others.}{
	A PC may attempt \skillCheck{15}{\skillStealth} to sneak to other immigrants an see how did they answer on the hardest parts of the Form.
}

\scriptedOption{Forge Signature.}{
	A PC who does not wish to give away all their information may attempt a \skillCheck{13}{\skillThievery or \skillDeception} to forge their signature or other data. 
}

\scriptedOption{Ask for Help.}{
	Good \skillCheck{16}{\skillPersuasion} may convince the scribe to help a person in a need of assistance.
}

\scriptedOption{Patient Work.}{
	The Form might be long, but surely, with just a little patience it can be solved. You can use \skillCheck{16}{\skillDiscipline} for that.
}


\subsection{\pursuitResolveHeader}

\pursuitReq{8 (or 10)}{4}

\scriptedOption{Success!}{
	The party completed their paperwork and got a permit to enter the city. Proceed to \fullref{sec:enteredCity}.
}

\scriptedOption{Failure...}{
	The azish law is too complicated and the form - needlessly complex. The PCs must find a different way to enter the city.
}
