\section{Running This Chapter}

This chapter describes party's interaction with the Cult of Secrets taking root in \locationYeddaw{}. One of their members, \npcBlindBeggar{}, will be the main antagonist of the story.

\subsection{Character Advancement}

The characters do not advance to the higher levels during this chapter. Optionally, if you believe the PCs spent long enough in \locationYeddaw{}, you may consider advancing them after \fullref{sec:2ndMeeting}, which should mark roughly the halfway of the story.

The following events may affect their path to radiance:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Sharing their secrets.
	\item Joining the cult.
	\item Following the law after finding the cult's hideout.
\end{itemize}


\subsection{Notable NPCs}

\npcBlindBeggar[s]{} is a \enemyCorruptedLightweaverC{}. He will meet the party several times during the story, until in the end he becomes the final boss of the adventure.

\roleplayBox{\npcBlindBeggar}{
	\roleplayTips
		{Secretive, provocative, uncanny}
		{To learn as many personal secrets of the PCs as possible.}
		{\npcBlindBeggar{} is bald and his eyelids are stitched with a thick cord, preventing them from opening. On his forehead he has a circular tattoo looking like a big eye made of scribbles. He always smiles in a mischievous way, suggesting he knows more than one would assume.}
		{As many members of the Cult, \npcBlindBeggar{} is a Lightweaver bonded to an Enlightened Cryptic who resides on his forehead like a big eye. They both love secrets and steal them from people whenever possible, often taunting them by suggesting how much they know.}
}
