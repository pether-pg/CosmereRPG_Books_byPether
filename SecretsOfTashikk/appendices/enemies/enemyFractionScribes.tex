\section{Azish Scribes}
\label{enemyFractionScribes}

Bureaucracy is a fundamental part of Azish empire, and paperwork forms run through it like blood in its veins. Scribes are an essential, and one of the most prestigious parts of the empire's infrastructure, bringing pride to anyone working in this noble profession.

Hardly any scribe in the Azish empire is allowed to use weapons offensively, but they have a right to self-defense should they be attacked. Because of that they will never be seen nearby a fight, unless they were tasked to prepare a post-combat protocol. \\

\tacticsBox{
	According to Azish Codex of Imperial Conduct, "Tactics" is a word used incorrectly in this context. Scribes do not follow tactics, they follow the law and perform their clearly defined duties.\vspace{0.15cm}
	
	\hspace*{0.3cm} In most cases, that would mean a scribe will guide others through the magnificent maze of the paperwork. In a Conversation, they will provide required forms to fill, possibly confusing the petitioner with Legal Technicalities. They will also tax any attempts of illegal actions.
	}
	

\subsubsection{Capable Bureaucrats}

All Azish scribes gain an utility expertise in Legal Codes and an advantage to all skill tests related to it - most often to the \skillLore{} tests. \\
