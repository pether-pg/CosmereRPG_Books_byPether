\setcounter{section}{-1}
\section{Running This Chapter}

Use scenes described in this chapter when the party breaks the law and must face the consequences of their actions.

\subsection{Character Advancement}

The characters do not advance to the higher levels during this chapter.

The following events may affect their path to radiance:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Visiting a lonely prisoner.
	\item Trading secrets with the guard.
	\item Helping friends to flee from jail.
	\item Following local law or accepting lawful punishment.
\end{itemize}
