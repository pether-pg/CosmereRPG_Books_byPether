\section{\locationYeddaw{} Prison}
\label{sec:prison}

When the PCs are arrested, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	You are stripped of your weapons and equipment and locked in a musty, smelly cell. On the wall, you see a note listing rights and regulations applying to people in custody. In the corner of your cell, there is another inmate sleeping.
}


The man already sitting in the jail is \npcAxiesCollector{}. He is in no rush to break from the prison, but he might help the PCs to escape.

\reasonBox{
	\npcAxies{} is a person who is expected to be found in places like this. However, if him being here does not fit the timeline of your story, feel free to replace him with another prisoner.
}


\subsection{Talking with the Prisoner}
\label{ssec:prisonTalking}

The prisoner does not initiate the conversation. However, if the PCs start talking with him, they will learn the following:

\scriptedOption{How did you get here?}{
	Oh, I was snooping in \locationIndicium{}, they didn't like that...
}

\scriptedOption{Do you know how to get out of here?}{
	\npcGossipGuard{} has a night shift tonight. She likes to gossip. Perhaps you will be able to convince her to release you.
}

\scriptedOption{Why didn't you escape?}{
	I lost a bet with my friend. I was sure they will not catch me. Now I must spend my time here.
}

\scriptedOption{Who is that friend?}{
	He is quite a philosopher. You must meet him, he would love to chat with you. When you get out, ask for \npcArclo{}, he can be found near the orphanage.
}

\scriptedOption{How long will you stay here?}{
	Oh, quite a time, but don't worry about it. Honestly, I'm used to it.
}

\ptrBox{
	Visiting the prison only to cheer up a lonely prisoner may be recognized by a cultivationspren.}

\subsection{Bringing the Cremling}

If \npcAxies{} sees that a cremling from \npcArclo{} travels with the party, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	"Hello, my friend", the inmate says, looking at the cremling you carry. "Well, you were right, I was not able to leave \locationIndicium{} unnoticed... But I did learn something before they captured me! One more set of shards is gone, drowned in the storm. And you were right about Stump, she isn't coming back anytime soon. But hey, I was right too! There is a Singer in the city, hiding among the immigrants! Someone stayed despite the exile regulations!"
}

\npcAxies{} reveals the secret he discovered in \locationIndicium{} before he was captured - there is a Singer in the city, hiding among the immigrants. He is being searched for, as the guards believe he might be dangerous and should be eliminated.

If the party knows their informant is a Singer, they might be able to connect the dots and follow that trail to find him. They can proceed to \fullref{sec:searchForSinger} as soon as they leave the prison.

\reasonBox{
	If the PCs got arrested quickly, eg. during a failed attempt to break into the city, do not serve them crucial information for free. However, if they failed all other attempts to learn about the location of \npcInformant{}, allow \npcAxies{} to share that information with them even if they don't have \npcArclo{}'s hordeling, or the party will not be able to progress.
}

\subsection{Gossiping with the Guard}

Once \npcGossipGuard{} starts her shift, the PCs may feed her some juicy gossip to intrigue her and perhaps persuade her to allow them to gather more details. You can run this scene as a Conversation.

\npcGossipGuard{} is a \enemyGuardB{}. \conversationDefence{She}{14}{15}{4} 
All tests to influence her gain disadvantage as she recognizes her integrity is at risk. However, she will need to spend 2 Focus against an attempt to influence her with a scandalous secret about someone well-known in \locationTashikk{}.

\conversationEnd{\npcGossipGuard{}}{1}


\subsubsection{Fabricating the Gossip}

The PCs may attempt to fabricate a false gossip if they do not wish to share the secrets they know. However, people in \locationTashikk{} can recognize false information. 

A PC must pass a \skillCheck{20}{\skillDeception} to use a lie to start the Conversation. On a failure, \npcGossipGuard{} will refuse to speak with the PC, forcing them to back down at the beginning of the Conversation, when another believable gossip is provided.

On a tier other than 2nd, the DC of the skill test equals to 12 + 4 $\times$ party's tier.


\newpage
\subsubsection{Resolving the Conversation}

The Conversation continues until \npcGossipGuard{} fails to spend Focus to resist the influence, or all PCs are forced to back down from it.

\scriptedOption{Success!}{
	The party convinces the guard to set them free, but she makes them promise to learn more details about the gossip and share them with her.
}

\scriptedOption{Failure...}{
	\npcGossipGuard{} cannot be manipulated into releasing the party. The PCs must find another way out.
}

\ptrBox{
	Sharing secrets with \npcGossipGuard{} may be recognized by a Cryptic.}


\subsection{Prison Break}

The party may prefer to do an old-fashioned prison break instead. If they do, you can run this attempt as an Endeavor. Set the DCs of the Endeavor tests to 13-16 (12 + 1d4), or 10 + 1d4 + 2 $\times$ tier of the party.

\pursuitReqPerPC{2}{1}

Potential attempts to contribute to the Endeavor may include:

\scriptedOption{Determine patrol patterns.}{
	Use \skillCheck{14}{\skillDeduction} to see if a PC can accurately determine the guard patrol path.
}

\scriptedOption{Creating a lockpick.}{
	To make a useful tool from bones and shells scattered around, a PC may attempt a \skillCheck{16}{\skillCrafting}.
}

\scriptedOption{Picking the lock.}{
	Using lockpick to open the jail may require a \skillCheck{15}{\skillThievery}.
}

\scriptedOption{Sneaking past the guards.}{
	Use \skillCheck{13}{\skillStealth} to determine if a PC can sneak unseen.
}

\scriptedOption{Taking an inaccessible path.}{
	Request \skillCheck{15}{\skillAgility{} or \skillAthletics{}} to determine if a PC can climb on a wall or take a leap into a safer area.
}

\subsubsection{\pursuitResolveHeader}

\pursuitReq{8}{4}

\scriptedOption{Success!}{
	The party retrieved their equipment and successfully escaped from the prison.
}

\scriptedOption{Failure...}{
	The PCs are caught and brought back to their cell. Each PC suffers 1d6 non-lethal damage after the meeting with the guards.
}

\ptrBox{
	Freeing the imprisoned may be recognized by a lightspren.}



\subsection{Unexpected Ally}

After both escape attempts failed, if the party managed to make some friends in the city before their capture, you can read the following:

\quoteBox{
	After several long hours of silence, suddenly you hear shouting and a commotion. Then, you see a body of a guard falling from the stairs, dead. Slow steps on the stone slabs follow afterwards. "I think this is enough time for you in here," you hear a voice speaking. "Get up, there is a work for you out there!"
}

Allow \npcArclo{} or \npcBlindBeggar{} to rescue the PCs if they believe it might benefit them. They will kill all the guards in the process, forcing Skybreakers to join future patrols chasing the party.




\subsection{Friends in High Places}

If nothing else allows for the escape, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	After several long hours, you hear steps on the stony stairs. A moment later, a stern guard approaches your jail. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"Hey, you! Get up!", he says to you. "Your superior provided the paperwork and paid the bail via Thaylen banking system. You are free to go. But if you cause problems ever again, you will be exiled from the city with no right for an appeal, you understand?"
}

Their patron persuaded them to release PCs from imprisonment. If they commit more infractions, they will be exiled from the city, and their mission here will fail.

\ptrBox{
	Accepting a just and lawful punishment may be recognized by a highspren.}


\subsection{Repeated and/or Violent Infractions}

Though the guards usually aim to arrest the party, if the PCs prove they cannot be contained or if they hurt a law representative, the guards will no longer believe the prison is the effective way to contain them.

Instead, the subsequent infraction will result in guards aiming to kill the party. If the guards fail, they will bring bigger force to the next attempt, asking Skybreakers for support if required.
