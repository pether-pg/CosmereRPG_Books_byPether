\section{Learning From Oprhans}
\label{sec:learnFromKids}

If the PCs failed to learn the secrets from \npcDoctor{}, they might wish to interview the children of the orphanage to learn more about its history. You may run this scene as an Endeavor.

\pursuitReqPerPC{1.5}{1}


\subsubsection{Example Contributions}

To complete the Endeavor, the PCs may do the tests listed below or propose their own suggestions. The DC of the tests should fall in the range of 13-16 (12 + 1d4) on tier 2, or (8 + 1d4 + 2 $\times$ tier) on other tiers.

\scriptedOption{Crafting a toy.}{
	A PC may use \skillCheck{13}{\skillCrafting} to make a fun toy for a kid to break the ice.
}

\scriptedOption{Organizing playtime.}{
	A PC may gather all children for games with a \skillCheck{16}{\skillLeadership}.
}

\scriptedOption{Playing tricks.}{
	A dexterous PC may attempt \skillCheck{15}{\skillThievery} to show some magic tricks.
}

\scriptedOption{Tell a story.}{
	A successful \skillCheck{14}{\skillLore{}} will allow a PC to recall some fairy tale to share with the kids.
}

\scriptedOption{Bribe with sweets.}{
	No skill test is required to earn a success if a PC prepared some sweets for the kids.
}

\reasonBox{Since this Endeavor focuses around talking with kids, you may consider rewarding good roleplay with advantages or even free successes.}

\subsubsection{\pursuitResolveHeader}

\pursuitReq{6}{4}
For each success, the PCs learn one of the facts below:

\begin{itemize}
	\item \npcArshqqam{}, the Stump, was running the orphanage before.
	\item \npcArshqqam{} was very stern and she always knew when people lied.
	\item \npcArshqqam{} healed many injured kids much faster than surgeons can.
	\item \npcArshqqam{} talked to herself very often. Some claim she talked to a shimmer of light on the wall.
	\item \npcArshqqam{} left after the red storm, claiming that the girl might know more.
	\item She probably went to Azir, following that reshi girl who was a friend of the emperor.
\end{itemize}

Once the PCs gather 6 facts from the orphans, they should be able to piece them together, allowing them to proceed to \fullref{ssec:stormshelterTradeSecrets}.

However, once the party gathers 4 failures, the kids lose interest with the conversation and refuse further answers. Perhaps the PCs can retry the next day...?

\ptrBox{
	Learning the truth from the children, unobstructed with conjectures and gossip of the surgeons, might be recognized by a mistspren.
}
