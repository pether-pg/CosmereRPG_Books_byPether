\section{Inside the City}
\label{sec:enteredCity}

When the characters enter the city of \locationYeddaw{}, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	Finally, you stand at the crowded street of \locationYeddaw{}. Everywhere around, you see the aftermath of the recent Everstorm. The city might be carved into the rock itself, but even that could not stop the destructive power of the raging storm.
}

Success! The PCs entered the city. As the first scene here, proceed to \fullref{ssec:enteredCityProhibitedPowers}.

\subsection{Prohibited Powers}
\label{ssec:enteredCityProhibitedPowers}

Just after the party entered the city, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	On the nearest wall, you see a piece of paper nailed with a thick bolt. The surface suffered during the recent storm, but most of the content is steal readable: \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"New law! By the will of Prince [tear] of \locationTashikk{} and majority of Law Tribunal votes, regulation MM-109 comes into force. All use of any magical, supernatural and/or void powers without a permit is strictly prohibited inside \locationTashikk{} borders. Any misconduct will be terminally curtailed by Shardbearer forces."
}

Surgebending and other supernatural powers are forbidden in the city. Failure to comply is punished by death. Make sure the PCs are aware of the law as soon as they are within the city borders, before they can do anything flashy.

Once it is clear, proceed to \fullref{ssec:enteredCityWanted}.

\subsection{City Patrols}
\label{ssec:enteredCityWanted}

Start this scene by reading the following:

\quoteBox{
	Even in a city this crowded, you cannot fail to notice armed guards patrolling the city. Many of them wear uniforms of \locationTashikk{} Guard, but they are heavily augmented with azish forces. As you watch them closer, you see one of the patrols reporting to a constable in an impeccable black uniform.
}

\locationYeddaw{} is protected by empire guards sent here by the emperor. They are supported by Skybreaker constables, should they meet more powerful enemies. Make sure to show those forces to the party.

If at any moment the party commits a crime while in \locationYeddaw{}, start \fullref{sec:wanted} event. Those crimes may include, but are not limited to: breaking into the city, failure to present valid documentation, assault on a city official, wielding of unnatural powers.

If the party entered the city illegally, start the event now. If not, they are safe. Either way, proceed to \fullref{ssec:enteredCityBeggar}.

\subsection{Blind Beggar}
\label{ssec:enteredCityBeggar}

For the first encounter in \locationYeddaw{}, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	Suddenly, you hear a rasping voice begging from below: "Please! Can you share something with a less fortunate?" As you look down, you see a man in ragged clothing, his eyelids stitched with a thick cord.
}

Beggar \npcBlindBeggar[s]{} is a \enemyCorruptedLightweaverC{}. He starts this scene with 0 Investiture and his goal is to get as many PCs' secrets as he can.

If the party interacts with the beggar, proceed to \fullref{sec:1stMeeting}.

\reasonBox{
	\npcBlindBeggar{} is the main villain of the story. Make sure to introduce him to the party early, but don't show all his powers yet. At this point, the goal of the scene with him is to foreshadow him and to make the players a bit uneasy.
}
