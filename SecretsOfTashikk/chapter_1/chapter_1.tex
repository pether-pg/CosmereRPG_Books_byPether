\section{Running This Chapter}

The story begins with the party entering \locationYeddaw{}, the biggest city in \locationTashikk{}, with hopes to get in touch with a mysterious informant holding crucial information.

\subsection{Character Advancement}

The characters should start this chapter at level 6-10 (tier 2). If the party is a higher or lower tier, adjust the tier of encountered adversaries. Do not advance the level of the party at the end of this chapter.

The following events may affect their path to radiance:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Submitting to local law and bureaucracy.
	\item Preventing authority figures from stopping them for puny reasons.
	\item Helping the immigrant.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Notable NPCs}

\npcHauka{} and \npcNissiqqan{} are performing their guard duty at the gates today. \npcNissiqqan{}, as a \enemyScribeNameB{} of Immigration allows \npcHauka{} do most of the work, but he sits nearby if she needs his expertise.

\roleplayBox{\npcHauka}{
	\roleplayTips
		{Scrupulous, dedicated, pragmatic.}
		{To serve the empire well as a guard captain.}
		{Wears a Azish guard uniform: boots, trousers, a cap, a breastplate and a cape.}
		{\npcHauka[s]{} is a woman who likes routine and order. She performs her duties with pride and integrity, ensuring order is maintained. She is know to often trade information for infused spheres to keep up her hobby of reading at night.}
}
