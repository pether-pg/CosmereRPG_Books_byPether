\section{Restoring Orphanage}
\label{sec:restoreOrphanage}

Once the PCs learn that without \npcArshqqam{} the orphanage is going to be replaced by a \locationKharbranth{} Hospital, they may wish to prevent that and restore the mission of the Orphanage.

You may run this attempt either as an Endeavor against a \locationYeddaw{} bureaucracy or a Conversation against a \enemyScribeC{}, depending on the party's approach. Either way, there is one requirement that the PCs must fulfill - they must find a person willing to run the orphanage.

There are no suitable candidates waiting in line for a job, so if the PCs wish to continue with the plan, one of them must volunteer to do it. When they do, allow them to complete the story, but after it is resolved they will need to stay back in \locationYeddaw{}.

\ptrBox{
	Stepping up into a role that is required to revive the closed orphanage may be recognized by a peakspren.
}
