\subsection{Final Conversation}
\label{ssec:3rdMeetingConversation}

\npcBlindBeggar{} is a \enemyCorruptedLightweaverC{}. \conversationDefence{He}{16}{20}{7}

His goal during this scene is to trade the secret he stole from \npcInformant{} for \lootSealedMessage{}. However, all secrets intrigue him and he will spend 2 Focus to resist influence of a test backed up with one.

During the Conversation, he will attempt to steal as many secrets from the PCs and use them to unnerve the party and cause the Focus loses.

\conversationEnd{\enemyCorruptedLightweaverC}{1}
The scene can also end by the party giving up their \lootSealedMessage{} - at this point, proceed to \fullref{ssec:3rdMeetingEnding}.



\subsubsection{Boss Conversation}

During this scene \npcBlindBeggar{} gains Boss (Conversation) feature, allowing him to be a formidable challenge for the party in this final scene of the story:

\abilityBox{\enemyStatblockFeatureBossConversation}

On top of that, once per scene, he can use his Firemoss ability to replenish his Focus reserves:

\abilityBox{
	\enemyStatblockAction[\iconActionDouble]{Firemoss}{This character grinds firemoss and inhales the smoke, regaining 6 Focus.}
}



\subsubsection{Boss Fight}

If the PCs attack \npcBlindBeggar{}, either to avenge \npcInformant{} or after they lose temper and back down from the Conversation, the scene changes into a Boss Fight. 

If \npcInformant{} is alive at this point, he will be slain at the first sign of PCs' hostility.

To even the odds, double the total Health of \enemyCorruptedLightweaverC{} and give him the Boss feature:

\abilityBox{\enemyStatblockFeatureBoss}

When \npcBlindBeggar{} drops to 10\% of Health, he will surrender, promising to share the informant's secrets in exchange for his life. If the PCs agree, proceed to \fullref{ssec:3rdMeetingEnding}.
