\section{Dramatis Person\ae}
\label{personae}

Following NPCs and fractions appear in the adventure:

\begin{itemize}	
	\item \npcDalinar[s]{} - Patron of the PCs. After recently receiving communication from \npcInformant{}, he sent the party with a mission to trade secrets with the informant. Does not appear in person in the adventure and can be switched for another patron.
	
	\item \npcInformant[s]{} - An informant who contacted the party's patron with offer to sell them some secrets.
	
	\item \npcImmigrant[s]{} - An immigrant attempting to enter \locationYeddaw{}, asks the PCs for help.
	
	\item \npcHauka[s]{} - Guard captain performing duty at her immigration post.
	
	\item \npcNissiqqan[s]{} - \enemyScribeNameB{} of Immigration, works with \npcHauka{}.
	
	\item \npcBlindBeggar[s]{} - Blind beggar and a \enemyCorruptedLightweaverNameC{}, the main villain of the story.
	
	\item \npcSjaAnat[s]{} - Unmade spren, secretly worshiped by \npcBlindBeggar{} and the Cult of Secrets. Referenced only, does not appear in person.
	
	\item \npcStormwarden[s]{} - Stormwarden at the stormshelter in which the party is supposed to meet with \npcInformant{}.
	
	\item \npcArshqqam[s]{} - She was previously running Tashi's Light Orphanage.
	
	\item \npcDoctor[s]{} - \locationKharbranth{}ian Surgeon, took over Tashi's Light Orphanage after \npcArshqqam{}'s departure.
	
	\item \npcArclo[s]{} - Sleepless philosopher, spends his time near the orphanage.
	
	\item \npcAxiesCollector[s]{} - Blue-skinned Siah Aimian, covered in tattoos. Is found in the local prison.
	
	\item \npcGossipGuard[s]{} - Guard in the \locationYeddaw{} prison, prone to gossip and secret trading.
	
	\item \npcGhenna[s]{} - \enemyScribeNameC{} in \locationIndicium{}.
\end{itemize}
