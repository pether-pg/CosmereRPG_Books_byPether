\section{Running the Adventure}
\label{sec:runningAdventure}

To run this adventure you will need \handbook{} containing all rules and guidelines on how to run \cosmereRPG{}. This book contains only adventure-specific details and suggestions on how to run it.

Whenever a character's name appears in \enemy{bold}, it means there is a corresponding stat block available. You can use it to get more info about the character's attributes and abilities.

Items appearing in \loot{bold italic} have special rules you can find at the end of this book.

If you are using this book in electronic version, it has also several quality-of-life improvements to make your life easier when you navigate this book:

All characters' names (e.g. \npcDalinar{}) are links that lead to \hyperref[personae]{Dramatis Person\ae} chapter, where all the NPCs are listed.

Unique items (e.g. \lootSealedMessage{}) and adversaries (e.g. \enemyScribeB{}) are also links that will lead you to the page with their unique rules. 


\subsection{Using with Other Adventures}

This adventure is expected to be used as a short stand-alone, or to be mixed with other stories as a side-quest adventure. You can play it after chapter 5 of \bookStonewalkers{}, around chapter 8 of \bookCotU{}, or adjust it to align with different adventures.


\subsection{Adjusting Difficulty}

This book assumes your players will form a party of 4 characters starting at tier 2 (levels 6-10). However, it is perfectly fine to have a different size of your group and/or to start playing at a different level.

If you see that the balance is a bit off, feel free to adjust the difficulty. You can change a number or tier of the enemies, modify endeavors' requirements or weave some other changes into the story.

To help you with that, this book will provide alternative stat blocks for the adversaries on different tiers, so you could easily change the tier of the adventure.



\subsection{Handling Canon}
\label{ssec:runningAdventureCanon}

Despite treating original source with respect, this adventure is not canon. While playing the game your primary focus should be fun. For everyone, "fun" means something different - either following official lore to the letter, creating your own touching story, or just hitting bad guys with your sword. 

Make sure you know what is fun for you and your group and make it your primary objective. If that means modifying this adventure or straying away from the canon - go for it!

You can find more information on that topic in “Canon and Continuity” section of \handbook{}.




\subsection{Playing with Singers}

The story takes place after the Everstorm, allowing for Singer characters to appear in the story. They might still attract more attention from human NPCs, but as long as you have a proper paperwork, the Azish and Tashikki people should not have a problem with you.



\subsection{Playing with Radiants}

During the events of the story, the Orders of Knights Radiant are reappearing and the spren are accepting the bonds again. There is nothing preventing you from playing as one of them. 

In fact, for the plot hook, the story assumes you are sent to \locationTashikk{} by \npcDalinar{}. However, you can change that part if it doesn't fit your plans.
