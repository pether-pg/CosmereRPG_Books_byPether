\section{Cult of Secrets}
\label{enemyFractionCorruptedLightweavers}

Cult of Secrets is a small organization worshiping \npcSjaAnat{} in a darkness of their hidden shrine, hoping to be rewarded with a Corrupted Cryptic to bond. 

Their hunger for secrets and hidden information drove them to \locationTashikk{}. Here, in the heart of \locationYeddaw{}, they can thrive, their hunger for secrets satiated.

\subsubsection{Blinding Creed}

The cult requires their members renounced their sight. At first, initiates wear blindfolds from dark material to cover their eyes, but once they bound with their spren and advance in the cult ranks, their eyelids are sown with thick cords or their eyes are gauged with a ritualistic dagger.

However, the lack of sight does not stop them from seeing. Instead, they quickly learn to use the surge of Illumination, which allow them to see the world around them. Perhaps the world they see is, in some way, even more true.

\tacticsBox{
	If possible, Cultists of Secret will attempt to avoid fighting. They recognize they are at a disadvantage, so they will disengage and attempt to disappear into the crowd using Illumination. \vspace{0.15cm} 
	
	However, they will never shy away from an opportunity for a conversation. They will use them to steal as much secrets from the other person. Some of those will be treasured for the future, other will be used to unnerve the opponent, threatening to reveal them.
}
