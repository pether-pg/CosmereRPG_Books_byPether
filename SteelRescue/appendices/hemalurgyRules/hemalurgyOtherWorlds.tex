\section{Hemalurgy on Other Worlds}
\label{sec:hemalurgyOtherWorlds}

Though never confirmed, it is theorized that Hemalurgy can steal powers originating from beyond the world of Scadrial. Work with your GM to confirm if that can happen in your games.

\subsection{Stealing Roshar Surges}
