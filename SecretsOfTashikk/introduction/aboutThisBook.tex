\subsection{Who is it for?}

While everyone is invited to have fun with this adventure, players who enjoy combat encounter the most may be disappointed by this story. By design, this module focuses on social encounters and it may contain not enough enemies to defeat with your sword.

\subsection{Is it canon?}

No. This is a fan-made campaign and the story presented in here cannot be considered canon. While I did my best to align with official lore of the world, some of the events here might contradict Brandon Sanderson’s vision.

\subsection{How many spoilers?}

This book may contain a few minor \bookED{} spoilers. However, those are not essential for the plot of the adventure and should be easy to modify away if you so choose.

\subsection{Motivation behind this book}

When I was GMing a campaign of Dungeons and Dragons for my friends, at one point they met Nothic. I find the idea behind the creature very original - it's an enemy who can steal your secrets and use them against you.

In a setting with magical healing, stolen secrets, reputation damage and long-lasting consequences of Nothic's machinations can be more dangerous than easily healed HP loss. I wanted to bring the same kind of challenge to \cosmereRPG{} and try to fit a Nothic-like enemy into the world of Roshar.
