\subsection{Who is it for?}

This module focuses on social encounters and, by design, avoids pushing the PCs into fighting scenes. While everyone is invited to have fun with this adventure, players who enjoy combat encounters the most may be disappointed by this story.

\subsection{Is it canon?}

No. This is a fan-made campaign and the story presented in here cannot be considered canon. While I did my best to align with official lore of the world, some of the events here might contradict Brandon Sanderson’s vision.

\subsection{How many spoilers?}

This book may contain a few minor \bookED{} spoilers. However, those are not essential for the plot of the adventure and should be easy to modify or remove if you so choose.

\subsection{Motivation behind this book}

When I was GMing a campaign of Dungeons and Dragons for my friends, at one point the players encountered a Nothic, a creature who steals secrets from a person and uses them against them- a concept I find original and interesting.

When magic can heal the most grievous of physical wounds, real danger is found in the machinations of a Nothic. Careless secrets and fragile reputations once lost, are not so easily recovered. I wanted to bring the same kind of challenge to \cosmereRPG{} and try to fit a Nothic-like enemy into the world of Roshar.
