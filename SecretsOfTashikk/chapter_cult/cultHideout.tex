\section{Cult Hideout}
\label{sec:cultHideout}

It is possible for the PCs to find the location of the mysterious Cult of Secrets. The PCs could be invited by \npcBlindBeggar{} during \fullref{sec:2ndMeeting}, or they could find some clues about it while proceeding with \fullref{sec:informationHeist}.

When the PCs reach this location, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	You enter a dark and musty cellar. The entire room is covered in darkness, with no candles or spheres anywhere nearby. Once you pull out your own source of light, you see a crude altar in the middle with a bloodied knife resting on top of it. Everywhere around, you see dozens of mirrors hanging on the walls. All of them are dusted, many are crooked, and some of them are broken, shards of glass scattered on the floor. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	At this moment, there are several people in the room. All of them are wearing ragged robes and have circular tattoos on their bald heads. One of them has his eyes gouged, other wear black blindfolds.
}

At this point, in the hideout there are four \enemyCorruptedLightweaverB{} and their leader - \enemyCorruptedLightweaverC{}. They regard the party with curiosity, but they are not hostile.

It is up to the PCs how they will handle this situation.


\reasonBox{
	The mirrors are here for \npcSjaAnat{} to appear and bless the cultists with her Enlightened children. It is up to you if she appears for the party, but this book does not cover this event.
}


\subsection{Purging the Cult}

The PCs may decide to free the city from the Cult's influence and to exterminate its members. You may run this as a Combat scene. If you believe the fight is too easy for the party, at the beginning of the 3rd round allow more \enemyCorruptedLightweaverB{}s to enter the scene.

By initiating the fight, the party risks that their actions will be deemed illegal, triggering \fullref{sec:wanted} event.

It is up to you if the PCs find anything interesting after the fight, but the cultists are rather poor individuals.
\newpage


\subsection{Joining the Cult}

If a PC agrees to join the cult, they will be treated in a friendly manner by other cult members. If the PC wears a blindfold on their eyes and follows the teaching of the cult leader, they will earn the title of \enemyCorruptedLightweaverNameA{}.

After some time, the \enemyCorruptedLightweaverNameA{} may attract an Enlightened Cryptic. If they proceed with the First Ideal, they will be recognized as a fully fledged \enemyCorruptedLightweaverNameB{}.

This book does not cover rules for progressing as an Enlightened Lightweaver. It is up to you how will you handle it. You may find suggested rules and talent trees under this link: \\

~~~~\url{https://github.com/pether-pg/CosmereRPG_Books_byPether/releases}

\ptrBox{
	Joining the cult may attract an Enlightened Cryptic. 
}

\subsubsection{Rewarded with Secrets}

After a PC joins the cult, start the event below. The party may spend one \iconOpp{} per scene to progress it. 

When the event triggers, the cult leader will inform the party that his cultists learned something that might help the PCs find the informant. He will share this information to the party as a reward for their loyalty. \\

\event{Rewarded with Secrets}{\iconOppEvent{}\iconOppEvent{}\iconOppEvent{}}{blueRadiance}



\subsection{Selling Cult's Secrets}

The cult's location seems to be kept in secret. The PCs might attempt to trade this information.

If the PCs try to sell this information at \locationIndicium{}, they will be able to exchange it for any information of a similar significance that is recorded there. That should make finding \npcInformant{} much easier.

When the PCs approach the city guard with this information, they will be offered 1000 mk for it. If the PCs agree, the guards will send a squad of \enemyGuardB{}s to eradicate the cult.

If the PCs approach Skybreaker constables with this information, they will send a couple of \enemySkybreakerC{} constables to eliminate any potential Surgebinders. After this assignment, they will thank the PCs, offering them to join their ranks.

\ptrBox{
	Allowing the local authority to handle criminal activity may be recognized by a highspren.
}
