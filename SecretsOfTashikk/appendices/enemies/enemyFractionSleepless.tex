\section{Sleepless Watchers}
\label{enemyFractionSleepless}

A Sleepless can be easily mistaken for a human - they practiced this ability for millennia. But don't be fooled by this mimicry - a sleepless can look like a man, but it is made of entire swarm of small, cremling-like hordelings. Driven by a single mind, the hordelings evolve, and adapt, and work together to reassemble a humanoid shape.

For an unsuspecting human, a sleepless would seem like a hideous, uncanny and deformed human, as hordelings not always mimic human body parts perfectly. With time and expertise, a sleepless might get better at posing as a human, but nevertheless - covering one's face helps to keep the appearance. For that reason, \locationYeddaw{} with their traditional shiqua wraps is a perfect hiding place for a sleepless.

Due to their nature, the sleepless are virtually immortal. Their most vital hordelings are always safely hidden, so even if someone managed to defeat one in combat, the sleepless will be able to regrow their horde with time. \\

\tacticsBox{
	\npcArclo{}, the only Sleepless in \locationYeddaw{}, does not seek to kill people. Instead, he prefers talking with them, asking deep questions, and work on his new philosophy. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	That doesn't mean he is defenseless. If attacked, the Sleepless will Overwhelm and Ravage his opponents. He does not care to preserve his Health, as his most important hordelings are safely hidden - even if he is defeated, he can always recreate his horde with time.
	}
