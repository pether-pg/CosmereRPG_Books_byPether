\label{chap:chapterInformant}

\setcounter{section}{-1}
\section{Running This Chapter}

In this chapter you can find the scenes in which the party are searching for the informant, \npcInformant{}.

\subsection{Character Advancement}

The characters do not advance to the higher levels during this chapter.

The following events may affect their path to radiance:
\begin{itemize}
	\item Trading secrets with \npcStormwarden{}.
	\item Learning stormwarden's trade.
	\item Securing information needed by the PCs' patron.
\end{itemize}


\subsection{Notable NPCs}

\npcStormwarden[s]{} is a stormwarden at the stormshelter where the party was told to search for \npcInformant{} the informant. He will help the PCs to find him.

\roleplayBox{\npcStormwarden}{
	\roleplayTips
		{Calm, stoic, reserved}
		{Have some fun while trading secrets}
		{Older, with graying hair. Has a scar on the side of the head made by one of the stones during a Highstorm.}
		{\npcStormwarden{} maintains one of the stormshelters in \locationYeddaw{}. Since this isn't the most exciting job, he likes to trade secrets as a hobby.}
}
