\chapter{Appendix \thechapter{}: Tables of Secrets}
\label{chap:secretsTable}

This adventure requires each PC to hide a few secrets. As the scenario does not include many Combat scenes, those secrets will provide a negative consequences for failing social encounters.

Each of the secret should be humiliating or incriminating enough that revealing them would be akin to social death of the PC, so the PC should feel pressure to protect them at any cost - just like they would protect their health pool.

Secrets included in this section can work as an inspiration for the players or as a randomly generated secrets for each PC. It is supposed to help you prepare to your game, but it is not mandatory to use exactly the secrets listed here.

You may notice that some of the secrets listed here are rather generic. This is intentional to apply to as many different PCs as possible. After picking such a secret, the player should work out the specific details for it.

\section{Personal Secrets}

Those secrets affect only one person close to the character. Revealing it will irreversibly shatter that person's trust, but other characters will probably ignore it. \\

\noindent
\begin{tabular}{p{0.5cm} p{7cm}}
	\color{black} d6 & \color{black} Secret \\ \hline
	
	\color{black} 1 & \color{black} I killed a beloved member of your family. \\
	
	\color{black} 2 & \color{black} During a gambling night, I lost your hand to some stranger. Now you must marry them. \\
	
	\color{black} 3 & \color{black} I broke the sacred oath I vowed to you. \\
	
	\color{black} 4 & \color{black} The things I told about my past are all lies. The truth is much darker... \\
	
	\color{black} 5 & \color{black} I broke/lost the object that was so important to you. \\
	
	\color{black} 6 & \color{black} I was never part of your team. I was sent to spy on you. \\
\end{tabular}

\section{Professional Secrets}

Those secrets, when revealed, would put into question the character's integrity, threatening their career and possibly resulting in excluding them from the group of their peers.

\noindent
\begin{tabular}{p{0.5cm} p{7cm}}
	\color{black} d6 & \color{black} Secret \\ \hline
	
	\color{black} 1 & \color{black} I do not believe in god and I don't respect people who do. \\
	
	\color{black} 2 & \color{black} I avoid doing my job, whenever possible. I blame any failures on others. \\
	
	\color{black} 3 & \color{black} My negligence caused the recent disaster. \\
	
	\color{black} 4 & \color{black} My loyalty is wavering, as I find service to the enemy more enticing. \\
	
	\color{black} 5 & \color{black} I have unprofessional relationship with one of my peers or their family. \\
	
	\color{black} 6 & \color{black} I am stealing the funds and selling the equipment for personal profit. \\
\end{tabular}




\section{State Secrets}

Highly guarded secret of the character's superior that the character knows either by involvement or pure luck. When revealed, will bring the wrath of the lord, treason accusation and potentially - death sentence. 

\noindent
\begin{tabular}{p{0.5cm} p{7cm}}
	\color{black} d6 & \color{black} Secret \\ \hline
	
	\color{black} 1 & \color{black} My lord hired an assassin to eliminate their rival. \\
	
	\color{black} 2 & \color{black} My lord is planning an invasion onto their neighbor's lands. \\
	
	\color{black} 3 & \color{black} Our researchers reached a breakthrough! The new discovery is kept secret. \\
	
	\color{black} 4 & \color{black} My lord schemes to betray their allay. \\
	
	\color{black} 5 & \color{black} My lord is scheming with the enemy. \\
