\section{Blind Nemesis}
\label{sec:3rdMeeting}

When the PCs reach the informant, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	In the dark alley, you see \npcBlindBeggar{} smiling with a wicked grin below his stitched eyes. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"I told you that Parshman were exiled from the city. One would say - killing one is a favor to all of us. But don't worry, I know all the secrets this one was hiding. Wanna trade?"
}

\npcBlindBeggar{} was faster and he reached \npcInformant{} before the party. However, depending on the Endeavor result in \fullref{sec:searchForSinger}, the informant might be still alive or already dead...

\reasonBox{
	Description in this scene assumes \npcBlindBeggar{} is a \enemyCorruptedLightweaverC{} wielding a Shardblade. If you are running this adventure on a lower tier, \enemyCorruptedLightweaverB{} carries no such weapon, so you will need to adjust the details.
}

\subsection{Singer Hostage}
\label{ssec:3rdMeetingHostage}

If the party succeeded during \fullref{sec:searchForSinger}, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	In front of the beggar, you see a terrified Singer with red patterns on the pale white skin, held hostage. On his shoulders, you see a Shardblade in the color of dark velvet. The edge of the blade looks like tears, scratches and scribbles welded together. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"You have the secret I crave", \npcBlindBeggar{} says. For the first time, you don't see his tattoo on his forehead. "I have something you want. Want to spare the pathetic life of this vermin? Or will you complete your mission? Choose!"
}

\npcInformant{} is still still alive and held hostage. \npcInformant{} offers to trade his life and secrets for \lootSealedMessage{}. Proceed to \fullref{ssec:3rdMeetingConversation}.



\subsection{Singer Slain}
\label{ssec:3rdMeetingMurder}

If the party failed during \fullref{sec:searchForSinger}, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	Behind the beggar, you see a body of a Singer lying on the ground. His eyes are burnt out and his clothes cut clean, but there is no blood to be seen. \vspace{0.15cm}
	
	"You have the secret I crave", \npcBlindBeggar{} says. "I have something you want. How much will you give me for it?"
}

\npcBlindBeggar{} killed the Singer after learning his secrets and now he wants the party to give him \lootSealedMessage{} in return for them. Proceed to \fullref{ssec:3rdMeetingConversation}.



\subsection{Final Conversation}
\label{ssec:3rdMeetingConversation}

\npcBlindBeggar{} is a \enemyCorruptedLightweaverC{}. \conversationDefence{He}{16}{20}{7}

His goal during this scene is to trade the secret he stole from \npcInformant{} for \lootSealedMessage{}. However, all secrets intrigue him and he will spend 2 Focus to resist influence of a test backed up with one.

During the Conversation, he will attempt to steal as many secrets from the PCs and use them to unnerve the party and cause the Focus loses.

\conversationEnd{\npcBlindBeggar{}}{1}
The scene can also end by the party giving up their \lootSealedMessage{} - at this point, proceed to \fullref{ssec:3rdMeetingEnding}.



\subsubsection{Boss Conversation}

During this scene \npcBlindBeggar{} gains the Boss (Conversation) feature, allowing him to be a formidable challenge for the party in this final scene of the story:

\abilityBox{\enemyStatblockFeatureBossConversation}

On top of that, once per scene, he can use his Firemoss ability to replenish his Focus reserves:

\abilityBox{
	\enemyStatblockAction[\iconActionDouble]{Firemoss}{This character grinds firemoss and inhales the smoke, regaining 6 Focus.}
}



\subsubsection{Boss Fight}

If the PCs attack \npcBlindBeggar{}, either to avenge \npcInformant{} or after they lose their temper and back down from the Conversation, the scene changes into a Boss Fight. 

If \npcInformant{} is alive at this point, he will be slain at the first sign of PCs' hostility.

To even the odds, double the total Health of \enemyCorruptedLightweaverC{} and give him the Boss feature:

\abilityBox{\enemyStatblockFeatureBoss}

\subsection{Aftermath}
\label{ssec:3rdMeetingEnding}

When the PCs reach this scene, read the following:

\quoteBox{
	"All right, here is the secret of this vermin", \npcBlindBeggar{} says. "There is no need to to do anything illegal, we are civilized people, are we not?"
}

\npcBlindBeggar{} is ready to share \npcInformant{}'s secret with the party. Proceed to \fullref{sec:missionComplete}.
