\section{Searching the Singer}
\label{sec:searchForSinger}

The PCs learned that \npcInformant{}, the informant they are looking for, is a Singer who has recently been seen in the Immigrant Quarter. They must search for him there. When they enter the district, read the following:

}

Finding a person who doesn't want to be seen in a Quarter this crowded will be a difficult task. You may run this scene as an Endeavor.

\pursuitReqPerPC{1.5}{1}

\subsubsection{Returning the Favor}

If the PCs helped \npcImmigrant{} to enter the city (see \fullref{ssec:cityGatesImmigrant}), he will recognize them in the Immigrant Quarter and offer them help. When he does, start the following event:\\

\event{Returning the Favor}{\iconOppEvent{}\iconOppEvent{}\iconOppEvent{}}{blueRadiance}\\

The PCs may spend \iconOpp{} from their rolls to progress the event. After the last one is acquired, the event triggers and \npcImmigrant{} manages to find \npcInformant{} and lead the party to him. Complete the Endeavor with a success.

\subsubsection{Example Contributions}

To complete the Endeavor, the PCs may do the tests listed below or propose their own suggestions. The DC of the tests should fall in the range of 13-16 (12 + 1d4) on tier 2, or (8 + 1d4 + 2 $\times$ tier) on other tiers.

\scriptedOption{Urban tracking.}{
	Tracking in a city is not much different from tracking in a wilderness and can be attempted with a \skillCheck{16}{\skillSurvival}.
}

\scriptedOption{Asking locals.}{
	Perhaps someone saw something? A \skillCheck{15}{\skillPersuasion{} or \skillIntimiation{}} could convince a person to share some information. That test gains an advantage if something substantial is offered for exchange for the secrets. 
}

\scriptedOption{Organized search.}{
	A PC may attempt a \skillCheck{14}{\skillLeadership} to rally the citizens of the Quarter for help.
}

\scriptedOption{Thinking like a refugee.}{
	Where would you go, if you were a Singer? A \skillCheck{14}{\skillInsight{} or \skillDeduction{}} could help a PC to determine that.
}

\scriptedOption{A better view.}{
	Perhaps if a PC climbed a little higher with a \skillCheck{15}{\skillAgility{} or \skillAthletics{}} they would have a better view?
}

\scriptedOption{Keen eye.}{
	When the Endeavor is close to completion, allow a PC to make a \skillCheck{13}{\skillPerception{}} to determine if they see anything interesting.
}



\subsubsection{\pursuitResolveHeader}

\pursuitReq{6}{4}

\scriptedOption{Success!}{
	The party finds their target! However, he is not alone... Proceed to \fullref{sec:3rdMeeting}, the informant is still alive!
}

\scriptedOption{Failure...}{
	The Singer is nowhere to be found... But the PCs cannot give up! They may continue the Endeavor, but when a PC makes a test to contribute to the Endeavor, that PC loses 1 Focus. After reaching the required number of successes proceed to \fullref{sec:3rdMeeting}, but the informant is already dead...
}
